`CalendarMonth`: a month of days, weeks from Monday, with today outlined and future days muted. `start` selects a day, `start` and `end` a range drawn as a band; picking a day emits `pick` with it, and the host decides. `locale` names the month (the browser's language by default), and `weekdays`, `previousLabel` and `nextLabel` are translatable. Each day is a button named by its full date.
