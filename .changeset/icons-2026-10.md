`Icon` draws 36 more icons from the set, so they no longer need an app icon: `lightbulb`, `square-pen`, `message-square`, `messages`, `message-quote`, `reply`, `send`, `image`, `images`, `captions`, `globe`, `webhook`, `external-link`, `calendar-check`, `calendar-x`, `calendar-clock`, `clock`, `history`, `split`, `merge`, `layers`, `undo-2`, `list-checks`, `maximize`, `minimize`, `panel-right`, `panel-right-close`, `sun-moon`, `sun`, `moon`, `circle-dashed`, `circle-half`, `circle-check`, `circle-alert`, `triangle-alert` and `key-round`. Common names resolve to them too, among them `thread` (messages), `collection` (layers), `focus` (maximize), `warning`, `error` and `success`. Icons an app adds with `setIconResolver` still win over the set.
