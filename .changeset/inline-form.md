`InlineForm`, a record's details with each value edited where it is, and its field editors `InlineFormInputText`, `InlineFormInputTitle` and `InlineFormInputSelect`: click or Enter to edit, Enter or leaving to save, Escape to undo. `fields` name their editor and pass it `props`; `change` reports the one field that changed.
