`EmptyState` takes `size="sm"` for an empty card in settings or a tray: an optional 16px icon in ink-muted, the `title` as one 13px muted line (a paragraph, not a heading), and the action as a small secondary button, in tighter padding. `description` isn't shown at this size. `md` stays the default and is unchanged.
