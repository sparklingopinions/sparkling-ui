Light and dark: `initColorMode({ storageKey? })` sets `data-mode` on `<html>` from the saved choice or the system's, and follows the system while on `system`; `useColorMode()` gives `mode`, `resolved`, `isDark`, `setMode` and `toggle`; `useForceLightMode()` keeps the page light while a component is mounted, overlays included.
