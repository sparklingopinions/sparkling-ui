`Tag` takes `pressable` to become a toggle chip for quick choices, bound to `v-model:pressed`, with `disabled`. It renders a `<button type="button" aria-pressed>`, 28px tall with 13px medium text: off on a surface with a line border, on in accent-wash with an accent-line border, accent text and a leading check. Group several in a `role="group"` with a label. A plain Tag is unchanged.
