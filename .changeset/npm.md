sparkling-ui is now open source under the MIT licence and published publicly to npm, so installing it needs no `.npmrc` entry or token. Remove the `@sparklingopinions:registry=https://npm.pkg.github.com` line and any `npm.pkg.github.com` token from your `.npmrc`, then reinstall. Storybook is at https://sparklingopinions.github.io/sparkling-ui/.
