`Avatar` (`name`, `image`, `size`, `shape`, `badge`, `hue`), `Chip` (`label`, `muted`), `Pill` (`tone` `neutral`, `accent`, `success`, `warning`, `info` or `danger`; `variant` `soft` or `text`; `dot`), `Tag` (`name`, `color`, `removable`, `removeLabel`, and a `remove` event) and `Kbd`. `hueFor(name)` gives the stable hue Avatar derives from a name. New colour roles: the categorical hues (`--sui-hue-<blue|green|orange|pink|purple|slate|red|amber>-<soft|line|ink>`) and `--sui-warning-bright`.
