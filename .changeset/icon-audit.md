Icons: 66 new, three renamed, and aliases only for other spellings of the same thing.

- Renamed, because the drawing showed something else: the pen nib is `pen` (was `document`), the page is `document` (was `building`), the euro sign is `euro` (was `funnel`). `building` and `funnel` have real drawings now. `posts` and `file-text` show the page; `company`, `companies` and `building-2` show the building.
- Removed aliases that named a different idea: `collection`, `library`, `stack` (all drew `layers`), `focus`, `sliders-horizontal`, `alt-text`, `scissors-line-dashed`, `flip-left`, `user` and `repeat`. `user`, `repeat`, `sliders-horizontal` and `scissors` are icons of their own now; use `maximize` for `focus`, `archive-restore` for `flip-left`.
- Removed `flip` and the old `mark-read` drawing (look-alikes of `reply` and `circle-check`). `mark-read` and `mark-as-read` now show `mail-open`; `person` shows `user`.
- `check` is drawn larger, in line with the arrows and `plus`.
- `link-2` is an alias of `link` (it was the same drawing).
- New: `archive-restore`, `arrow-left-circle`, `arrow-up-circle`, `bell-off`, `book-open`, `bookmark`, `briefcase`, `bug`, `building`, `calendar-plus`, `camera`, `chart-pie`, `chevron-up`, `circle-plus`, `circle-x`, `clipboard`, `clipboard-check`, `cloud`, `cloud-upload`, `columns`, `credit-card`, `database`, `file-plus`, `folder`, `folder-open`, `folder-plus`, `funnel`, `gift`, `hash`, `languages`, `lock-open`, `mail-open`, `map-pin`, `megaphone`, `menu`, `mic-off`, `minus`, `monitor`, `newspaper`, `panel-left`, `panel-left-close`, `paperclip`, `pause`, `percent`, `pin`, `power`, `receipt`, `repeat`, `rss`, `scissors`, `share`, `shield-check`, `sliders-horizontal`, `smartphone`, `star`, `stop`, `tag`, `target`, `terminal`, `thumbs-down`, `thumbs-up`, `user`, `user-plus`, `video`, `wallet`, `zap`.
